What is GDACS?

The Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System is a cooperation framework between the United Nations and the European Commission. It gives disaster managers worldwide real-time access to web-based disaster information systems and related coordination tools.

Rescura Sync reads the GDACS real-time feeds directly, parsing automated estimates and risk analysis for sudden-onset disasters from the European Commission Joint Research Centre — which is what lets it route dispatch and logistics to the nearest supply depot.
