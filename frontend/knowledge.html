Knowledge base

Methodology behind Rescura Sync: the humanitarian standards, spatial formulas, machine learning models, and routing algorithms it runs on.

Humanitarian Standard The Sphere Project Minimum Standards

Rescura Sync aligns all emergency supply predictions with the internationally recognized UN Sphere Humanitarian Charter:

  • Water Allocation: 20 Liters per person per day (covering 3-5L drinking hydration, 5L cooking, and 10-12L sanitation and disease prevention).
  • Food Rations: 3 emergency food packs per person per day (delivering at least 2,100 kcal of daily fortified nutrition).
  • Medical Trauma Kits: 1 full emergency response kit per 150 affected individuals.
  • Severity Multiplier: Supply volumes automatically scale upward when disaster severity exceeds 5.0/10 to account for damaged municipal infrastructure and contaminated water sources.
Spatial Mathematics Great-Circle Distance & 50km Radius Engine

Real-world curved distance between supply hubs and disaster coordinates is calculated using the spherical Haversine Distance Method (using Earth's radius of 6,371 kilometers):

  • Curvature Precision: Converts differences in Latitude and Longitude into true ground kilometers across Earth's curved surface, avoiding flat-map distortions.
  • 50 km Immediate Impact Radius: Automatically aggregates census populations from all townships within a 50 km perimeter of the disaster epicenter.
  • Rural Distance Decay: If a crisis strikes remote hills or offshore waters without a major city inside 50 km, an inverse distance decay model estimates surrounding rural population so remote communities are never left with zero aid.
Multi-Modal Logistics 3-Zone Operational Routing & Airlift Hierarchy

Logistics dispatch automatically recognizes geographic boundaries and assigns realistic transport modalities:

  • Zone 1 (Domestic Myanmar, under 600 km): Dispatches tactical ground convoys (50 km/h), rescue helicopters (220 km/h), or delta rescue boats (25 km/h) from Yangon, Naypyidaw, or Mandalay depots.
  • Zone 2 (Regional ASEAN, 600 to 1,800 km): Dispatches regional C-130 tactical airlifts (500 km/h) or cross-border highway corridors from AHA Centre regional stockpiles.
  • Zone 3 (Global / Inter-Continental, over 1,800 km): Automatically marks Land and Water transit as N/A (infeasible across oceans) and deploys Strategic Long-Range Jet Cargo Airlift (800 km/h) coordinated via UNHRD and UN-OCHA global reserve networks.
Machine Learning AI Multi-Output Random Forest Model

To predict operational timing and logistics demand beyond static formulas, Rescura Sync runs a trained ensemble Random Forest AI Regressor:

  • Input Parameters: Disaster Latitude, Longitude, and Severity Score (1 to 10).
  • Simultaneous Outputs: Required Water Liters, Required Food Packs, and Estimated Rescue Completion Time (in Hours).
  • Performance & Reliability: Model executes inference in under 30 milliseconds without requiring heavy external GPU libraries, and is immune to tabular data overfitting.
System Architecture Real-Time Telemetry & Zero-Downtime Fallback

Built for continuous operational resilience under extreme disaster field conditions:

  • GDACS & USGS Live Streams: Telemetry feeds are cached with stale-while-revalidate protection to provide instant sub-5ms responses while updating in the background.
  • Dual-Engine Resilience: Connects to cloud PostgreSQL (Supabase) and automatically falls back to embedded local SQLite with zero downtime if internet or satellite links drop.
  • Multi-Dispatcher WebSockets: When a commander locks and dispatches supplies, real-time WebSocket frames notify all active screens in under 10 milliseconds to prevent duplicate dispatches.
Reporting Engine Automated Action Plan PDF Export

Command personnel can generate comprehensive, citable operational action plans with a single click:

  • High-Speed PDF Generation: Renders mission summaries, assigned supply depots, transit ETAs, and humanitarian requirements in under 200 milliseconds.
  • Instant Download: Ready-to-print executive briefs accessible directly from both the interactive map popup and the master disaster events table.
