Knowledge base

Methodology behind Rescura Sync: the humanitarian standards, spatial formulas, machine learning models, and routing algorithms it runs on.

Humanitarian Standard The Sphere Project Minimum Standards

Rescura Sync aligns all automated supply calculations with international humanitarian standards established by The Sphere Charter for disaster relief operations:

  • Water Requirements: 15 Liters per person per day (covering drinking, personal hygiene, and basic food preparation).
  • Food Allocation: 2,100 kcal daily nutritional intake per person, packaged into 1.2 kg high-density emergency food packs.
  • Vulnerability Multipliers: Operations automatically apply a 1.25x scaling factor for severe crisis zones (Severity ≥ 7.0) to account for infrastructure damage and supply disruption.
Spatial Mathematics Haversine & 50km Sphere Engine

Geographic proximity between supply depots and disaster epicenters is calculated using great-circle spherical distance via the Haversine Formula:

d = 2r · arcsin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ ¹)cos(φ ²)sin²(Δλ/2)))

Our 50km Sphere Engine draws a 50 km radial buffer around active epicenters to evaluate population density, road connectivity, and vulnerable civilian sectors.

Machine Learning Multi-Target RandomForest AI Model

To predict complex operational needs beyond linear formulas, Rescura Sync trains an ensemble RandomForestRegressor machine learning model on historical operation records:

  • Input Features: Severity Score (1-10), Estimated Population at Risk, Latitude, Longitude, and Proximity to Logistics Hubs.
  • Predicted Targets: Required Water Volume (Liters), Food Packs Required, and Estimated Operation Rescue Time (Hours).
  • Model Weight Persistence: Model weights are persisted in rescue_logistics_model.joblib for fast inference under 50ms.
Network GIS OSMnx Road Graph & Routing Engine

Evacuation routes and convoy dispatch paths are generated dynamically by pulling real-world road networks from OpenStreetMap using OSMnx:

  • Graph Processing: Converts road segments into a directed graph with travel impedance based on speed limits and surface quality.
  • Dijkstra Optimization: Computes optimal dispatch routes from the nearest registered supply depot (Yangon Central, Mandalay Hub, Naypyidaw Reserve) to the disaster target.
Telemetry Ingestion GDACS RSS XML & Supabase WebSockets

Live emergency events are ingested asynchronously from the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS):

  • 5-Minute Ingestion Cache: Fast in-memory caching prevents API rate limits while ensuring real-time emergency responsiveness under 100ms.
  • Civilian SOS Signals: Encrypted WebSocket distress feeds from mobile apps push live coordinates and emergency needs to command personnel.
PDF Engine fpdf2 Automated Action Plan Export

Command commanders can generate C-level executive action plan reports with a single click:

  • fpdf2 High-Precision Rendering: Renders executive summaries, dispatch route telemetry, depot allocations, and emergency contact lists into PDF format.
  • Instant Download: Served directly from FastAPI backend endpoint /api/export-report/{id} as downloadable PDF documents.
